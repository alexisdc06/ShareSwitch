## Maker comment (post the second the listing goes live)

> Hi Product Hunt 👋 I'm Alex — industrial and UI/UX product designer, founder of
> AC Design Studio. I build and prototype in public on A Life of Design.
>
> I made ShareSwitch out of a very specific frustration. In every design review I'd
> share one window, then need to show another: stop sharing, hunt for the right
> thumbnail, share again, "can you see it now?" Ten seconds of dead air, every time,
> and sometimes a glimpse of something I didn't mean to show.
>
> ShareSwitch fixes it with one idea: you share a single ShareSwitch window once, and
> it mirrors whatever window you point at. Hold Ctrl+Alt, click any window, and that's
> what your call sees. You can also save windows to number keys and jump between them.
>
> It's free and open source (MIT), needs no account or admin rights, and has no network
> code at all — the privacy page explains exactly what the keyboard shortcut can and
> can't see.
>
> What it doesn't do yet: show your mouse cursor in the mirror, and run on macOS.
>
> I'd really value brutal feedback, especially from people who present a lot. What would
> stop you using this every day?
